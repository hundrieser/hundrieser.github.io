I am a postdoctoral researcher at Yale University hosted by <a href="http://www.stat.yale.edu/~hz68/"> <b>Harrison H. Zhou</b></a> and <a href="http://www.stat.yale.edu/~yw562/"> <b>Yihong Wu</b></a>. Prior to that I was postdoctoral researcher at University of Twente in the group of <a href="https://jschmidthieber.personalweb.utwente.nl/"> <b>Johannes Schmidt-Hieber</b></a>. Both stays are being funded by a <a href="https://www.leopoldina.org/en/funding/leopoldina-fellowship-programme/leopoldina-postdoc-scholarship/"><b>Leopoldina Postdoctoral Scholarship</b></a>. 

I study statistical structure in modern learning systems. My work spans **statistical optimal transport**, **statistical inverse problems**, and the **statistical theory of neural networks**, with earlier contributions to **statistics on non-Euclidean spaces**. 

 Selected works are outlined below; a full list of my publications is detailed <a href="https://hundrieser.github.io/publications/"><b>here</b></a>.
